an.audio.voice_design#

Design a project’s voices from their written notes, then let the writer choose.

A voice document (assets/voices/<ref>.json) may carry the voice in words: notes (age, accent, pitch, pace, attitude) and, optionally, sample_text (what the samples say). Voice design turns those words into candidate voices the writer can LISTEN to, and the writer’s choice becomes the document’s voice_id. That is how a regional accent gets cast by the one person who can hear it (a local ear for a local accent), with nobody else picking from a stock library.

Two steps, both plain functions over injected stores:

  • design_voice_options(): per voice, ask a designer for previews of its notes speaking its sample text, store each preview’s audio in samples and return the options manifest ({ref: {"notes", "sample_text", "design_model", "options": [{"option": "A", "generated_voice_id", "voice_id", "sample", "duration_s"}]}}). With persist=True (the default) every preview is saved as a durable provider voice at once, because a provider’s previews expire; with persist=False only the chosen one is, later (fewer voices in the account, but a choice must come before the previews expire).

  • choose_voice_option(): the voice_id of the chosen option (saving it as a voice first when it was not persisted).

  • choose_in_entry(): the choice made in one manifest entry (that voice_id, the designed voice it replaces retired, who chose it and when), returning the voice document and the replaced voice: the step every chooser shares, the CLI’s choose_project_voice() and a connector’s alike.

Two more routes to a voice (an#485). Design from notes can miss a regional accent entirely (every design of “rural Limerick Irish” came out English). So a voice document may also carry a library query ({"accent": "irish", "gender": "male", "age": "middle_aged"}), or an voices design --library accent=irish,gender=male gives one to every voice: the provider’s shared voice library, human-tagged by region, is searched and its candidates join the same manifest as L1, L2, … options (ElevenLabsVoiceLibrary behind the library= seam; a search and a preview cost nothing). And an voices audition makes an audition reel per option: the character’s own lines from the scene, spoken with the film’s TTS provider and model, the line’s cues as audio tags, 0.6 s apart (audition_voice_options(), the tts= seam), so the writer hears what the film will sound like, accent drift of the render model included. The writer’s ear is the judge: an accent classifier was measured and cannot screen TTS accents (an#485).

A choice is recorded, and a render needs one (an#486). A voice written in words (notes) is cast by the writer, nobody else: an voices choose writes chosen_by and chosen_at beside the voice_id (record_choice()), and a voice document that has notes but no recorded choice is a render finding, refused under --strict-assets before anything is synthesized (voice_casting_problem()). A timing draft may speak a stand-in voice only with an explicit provisional (true, or a string saying why), which every render report lists.

The project-level forms, design_project_voices() and choose_project_voice(), keep the manifest in artifacts/voice_options/<ref>.json, the samples in artifacts/voice_samples/<ref>_<option>.mp3, and write the chosen voice_id (and provider) into the voice document. an voices design and an voices choose are their CLI.

The designer is a seam (designer=): by default ElevenLabsVoiceDesigner (ElevenLabs Voice Design through the elevenlabs SDK an’s ElevenLabs TTS already uses; key in ELEVEN_API_KEY). voxy’s ElevenLabsSpeechModel is a drop-in (designer=voxy.get_speech_model("elevenlabs")), not the default because voxy brings torch. Anything with these two methods works:

design_voice_previews(description, *, text, model_id) -> [preview]   # preview.generated_voice_id, .audio, .duration_s
design_voice(description, *, preview, name) -> profile                # profile.segment is the voice id

Designing is billed by the provider; nothing here is called by a render.

>>> class Fake:
...     def design_voice_previews(self, description, *, text=None, model_id=None):
...         from types import SimpleNamespace as N
...         return [N(generated_voice_id=f"g{i}", audio=b"ID3", duration_s=1.0) for i in range(2)]
...     def design_voice(self, description, *, preview, name):
...         from types import SimpleNamespace as N
...         return N(segment="v-" + getattr(preview, "generated_voice_id", preview))
>>> samples = {}
>>> notes = "A man in his fifties from rural Yorkshire, warm and unhurried."
>>> manifest = design_voice_options({"jerry": {"notes": notes}}, samples=samples, designer=Fake())
>>> [(o["option"], o["voice_id"], o["sample"]) for o in manifest["jerry"]["options"]]
[('A', 'v-g0', 'jerry_A'), ('B', 'v-g1', 'jerry_B')]
>>> sorted(samples)
['jerry_A', 'jerry_B']
>>> choose_voice_option(manifest["jerry"], "B", designer=Fake())
'v-g1'

an.audio.voice_design.choose_in_entry(entry, option, *, by=None, designer=None, ref='', at=None, doc=None, in_use=(), name_prefix=None)[source]#

Make option the choice of one voice’s options manifest entry (an#549): the one choose step an’s an voices choose and a connector (reelee’s production pack) share. Returns the voice document (record_choice() of doc, default an empty one: a caller that keeps the voice file elsewhere merges these keys into it) and the voice it replaced.

entry is updated in place: chosen records the option, its voice_id, chosen_by and chosen_at. Choosing the option already chosen reuses its voice (nothing is saved), and keeps its chosen_at when the same chooser chooses it again and no at is given. Choosing another one names its voice (saved as a provider voice when it was not, choose_voice_option(), named under name_prefix, default the entry’s, and marked saved_by_choice and saved_under that prefix on its option) and retires the voice it replaces when a choice saved it: its option no longer names it, and entry["retired"] records it ({voice_id, option, name_prefix}, the prefix it was saved under, an#618), never deleted here (a copy of the manifest, or a voice file already written, may still name it: freeing the provider’s slot is a separate, explicit act). A voice saved when the options were designed (persist=True), a library voice and one cast by hand stay named where they are; a choice carried over a re-design (retire_on_replace, an#604) is retired when replaced. A voice in in_use (one another voice document speaks) is never retired: pass the project’s other voices. by defaults to the login name (see record_choice()). A host choosing for a project of its own (a fork) passes its name_prefix rather than re-stamping the entry’s, which names the voices the entry already saved.

>>> from types import SimpleNamespace
>>> class Saver:   # a designer that saves a preview as voice "v<preview>"
...     def design_voice(self, notes, *, preview, name): return SimpleNamespace(voice_id="v" + preview)
>>> entry = {"notes": "n", "options": [{"option": "A", "generated_voice_id": "a"},
...                                    {"option": "B", "generated_voice_id": "b"}]}
>>> choose_in_entry(entry, "a", by="noel", at="t1", designer=Saver()).document["voice_id"]
'va'
>>> choice = choose_in_entry(entry, "B", by="noel", at="t2", designer=Saver())
>>> choice.previous, choice.retired, [o["voice_id"] for o in entry["options"]]

't2'
