an.stage.world#

The stage’s side of the scene world (ADR 0009): measuring a member’s extent, and lowering a whole scene for the compiler.

The IR layer (an.ir.world) projects points and sizes; what it cannot know is how big an entity’s art is. This module measures that the way the runtime would draw it, without a browser: it compiles the entity’s specimen shot alone and takes the union of its nodes’ boxes (an.stage.timeline.node_box(), an#445), relative to the entity’s own placement point. Any registered entity kind with a specimen is measured the same way, so a genre’s rig and the core’s props get one rule.

lower_scene() is the one call the pipeline’s entry points make (an render, an preview, an probe, an validate, the orchestrator): extents, film starts, then an.ir.world.lower_world().

an.stage.world.lower_scene(scene, mall, *, fps=None, film_starts=None, project_dir=None, continues=True)[source]#

scene as the compiler should see it: every framed shot’s placements derived from the world (an.ir.world.lower_world()), with the members’ extents measured here and each shot’s film start from the assembled timeline (so a travel’s streams keep phase across cuts). A scene without a world comes back as it is, at no cost. Then every shot’s continue: is resolved (an.continuity.continue_shots(), an#542): a shot that continues another opens as that one ends (project_dir: where a sibling scene file is read; default the mall’s; continues: off for the world alone, as an validate reports the two apart).

Raises an.ir.world.WorldError for a viewpoint that cannot be resolved; an validate reports the same error instead of raising.

an.stage.world.seed_world(scene, mall, shot_id, *, focal=None, depth=None)[source]#

scene with a world derived from its hand-placed shot shot_id (an.ir.world_from_shot.seed_members(), an#514): the members, and one viewpoint named after the shot, a camera at the origin with focal (pixels; default: the default field of view, so the world frames the same at any resolution). The shot names that viewpoint and keeps its own stage lines, which agree with the world. The art is measured here, as the lowering measures it (native_box(); a measurement that fails leaves the entity out, said in skipped).

Raises ValueError for a scene that already has a world, an unknown shot or one that already names a viewpoint, and what seed_members() refuses.
