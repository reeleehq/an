an.stores.characters#

Characters store — descriptor + sidecar folder per character.

A character’s character.json is the Phase 11a CharacterDescriptor (slot/skin/animation graph + viseme map). Binary parts (SVGs, PNGs) are sidecars under the same directory at e.g. parts/head.svg, parts/mouth/mouth_a.svg, etc.
