an.verify.support#

The support and skate checks: a body declared to rest on a surface does, and its feet do not slide on it (an#519, catalogue A1 and B3).

Two constraints on the one interface (an.verify.constraints.Constraint), lint-only (no solver term in v1), over the compiled shot, measured by the pure function an.stage.support.supports_at(). support (order 252):

  • a declared support joint ({type: support, a: paul, b: ground}) whose contacts sink into its surface (penetration) or whose lowest contact is above it (floating) by more than an.stage.support.support_tolerance() of the entity’s height, at any frame of the solve’s grid at which it is joined, is an error span; a reference it cannot resolve is an error too (the solve never sees a support, so unknown_body cannot say it);

  • an entity nothing declares, taken to stand on the shot’s floor (solve: {ground: y}, else the world’s under it, World.ground) as it opens, is held to it at its rest instants (between two keys where nothing of it moves), as a warning;

  • b: ground on a shot with no floor is measured against where the entity stands as the shot opens, said once as info.

Not judged: an unjoined span (unjoin suspends the support: it is not a flight), an entity’s declared flight (overrides.airborne), an item the hold layer moves when nothing declares a support on it (floating_item’s).

skate (order 253, catalogue B3 i): a limb’s contact (one whose node is not the entity’s own nor one of its root parts: a foot on a leg hinged to the body) held on its surface (its support joined, within the support tolerance) is foot skate when, between two instants of the grid at which it is held, its foot slides along the surface faster than an.stage.support.skate_tolerance() (a fraction of the entity’s height per SECOND, so one slide gets one verdict at 24 or 60 fps), or when, over one stance, it drifts from where it was planted by more than an.stage.support.skate_drift_tolerance() (a slow skate adds up). A step on a step_hz grid is reported per step, not per frame. A step whose foot moves along the surface’s normal by more than an.stage.support.SKATE_LIFT of its slide is a lift-off or a landing, not measured. The slide is measured in the surface’s own frame (a body’s surface: in the body’s own units from its first end, so a figure standing on a pushed or mirrored crate does not skate; the floor: from the stage’s origin). An error for a declared support; for an entity taken to stand on the floor, a warning, and only under a style pack that declares gravity (a cartoon style that glides is right by declaration). A body’s own stance point (origin, the ink of its roots, a prop’s base, a bone on a root part) never skates: a pushed crate or a gliding figure slides on it by right. In a shot a world frames, ground is the world’s floor under each member when the world declares one (World.ground, an#519 PR 4) and the shot no solve.ground: the floor line at the member’s depth as the camera projects it, and the slide measured from the member’s opening ground point held in the frame it stands in, so a member carried by a moving frame does not skate. A member whose frame moves relative to the viewpoint’s and that has no world floor is not judged on ground (a stage floor does not stay under it): said once as info.
