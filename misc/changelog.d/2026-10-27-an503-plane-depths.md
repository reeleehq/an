- **A plane at a world depth** (an#503, slice 1): an environment member's `depths: {plane: z}` (world units) derives the plane's parallax factor `z_ref / z` (`z_ref`: mean depth of what the shot shows); the lowering hands it to the compiler as `overrides.plane_factors`, which compiles byte for byte as the same factor written on the plane. A depth plus the plane's own `depth`/`parallax`, an unknown plane, or a depth on a plane in a `far` frame is a validate error (the compiler refuses the first). Not yet: a plane in a frame that moves relative to the viewpoint's (sampled motion keys), the dolly's apparent size.
