- `an.audio.voice_design.choose_in_entry` is the one choose step for a voice options manifest entry (an#549), shared by `an voices choose` and a connector: it returns the voice document, the voice the choice displaced and the one it retired. Only a voice a choice itself saved (marked `saved_by_choice`) is retired into the entry's `retired` ledger (never deleted); voices saved at design time, library voices and hand-cast voices stay named. The same choice again keeps its first `chosen_at`.
