- **Transcript references are lacing's** (an#670): `an.stores.transcripts` parses, formats and reads `artifact:[<store>/]<key>[@<sha256>]` through `lacing.ArtifactRef` / `resolve_in_mall` (lacing#90) instead of its own copy; `parse_artifact_ref` and `ARTIFACT_REF_PREFIX` are gone (use `lacing.ArtifactRef.parse(...).locate(...)` and `lacing.ARTIFACT_REF_PREFIX`). Refusals still name the missing part (store, key, content, pin); a pin in capitals is now a pin, text without `artifact:` is refused instead of sliced, `artifact:takes/` names key `takes/` of the transcripts store, and a transcript key a reference could not name (whitespace, a leading `/`) is refused when recorded.
