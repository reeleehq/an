- **A follow that dollies** (an#625): `camera.follow` takes `camera.keys` setting only `dolly` and `zoom` as an overlay on its sampled path; the follow pass evaluates them at its frames (`an.ir.camera.follow_overlay`) and places the camera at `d − d0·g0/g`, so it closes in on a moving target while holding it on screen. A key that also sets `x`/`y`/`rotation` beside a follow is refused; a follow with no overlay is byte-identical; validate checks the overlay's travel against the planes, and an axis the follow does not track zooms about where the target started on it.
