- **Solved channels drop the keys they do not need** (an#636, item 2, partial): `an.stage.solve.compact_keys` keeps a solved key only where linear interpolation between the kept ones strays more than `SOLVED_KEY_TOLERANCE_PX` (0.001 px per channel; a rotation at the puppet's whole reach), so still and evenly moving stretches lose theirs: a dynamic regrip shot's document shrinks from 24.6 to 10.8 KB, while curved motion keeps its keys. A point moves at most the tolerance × (√2 + its chain depth) × its scale.
