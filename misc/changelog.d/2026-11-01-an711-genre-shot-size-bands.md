- **A genre gives its own shot-size bands** (reeleehq/cutan#106): `EntityKind.shot_sizes(entity, mall, box) -> {size: (top, bottom)}` lets a kind say where its rig puts each shot size; `an.stage.world.member_shot_sizes` measures them and `lower_world`/`resolve_viewpoint` frame a subject by its own band (`SHOT_SIZES`' otherwise, and always its `fill`). One it cannot give (a hook that raises, a malformed or out-of-range band, an unknown size) is not used and is a `shot_size` note on each shot that frames it. `an.genres.API_LEVEL` 14.
