- **A genre gives its own shot-size bands** (reeleehq/cutan#106): `EntityKind.shot_sizes(entity, mall) -> {size: (top, bottom)}` lets a kind say where its rig puts each shot size; `an.stage.world.member_bands` measures them and `lower_world`/`resolve_viewpoint` frame a subject by its own band (`SHOT_SIZES`' otherwise, and always its `fill`). A band outside 0 <= top < bottom <= 1 or a hook that raises is not used.
