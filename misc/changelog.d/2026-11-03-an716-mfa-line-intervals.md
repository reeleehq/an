- A plain transcript of several lines in one take, aligned by MFA, is first timed roughly by the transcriber, then each line aligned within its own interval (a TextGrid of intervals, `LineAligner.align_lines`), as MFA is built for short utterances; with no transcriber, or rough words that do not fit the lines, it falls back to one utterance with a `RecordedVoicesWarning`, a line MFA leaves without words keeps its rough times and is warned of, and `rough_timing=False` (`an voices import --no-rough-timing`) never transcribes; `an.audio.transcripts.textgrid_text` writes a TextGrid, refusing intervals out of span or overlapping (an#716).
