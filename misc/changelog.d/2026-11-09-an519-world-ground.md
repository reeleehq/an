- **`World.ground`, the world's floor** (an#519 PR 4, catalogue A1/B3): a world may declare `ground: y`, the world `y` of a horizontal floor plane; the lowering writes the floor under each positioned member as the camera projects it at its depth (`overrides.ground`, `an.base.GROUND_OVERRIDE`; keyed per frame and thinned for a member the camera sees move, with its position along the floor in world units in the frame it stands in; read by `an.ir.world.world_ground_at`); in a framed shot with no `solve.ground`, a `support` to `ground` is measured against it, an entity standing on it is taken to stand, and `skate` measures a foot along it in the member's own frame, so a passenger of a moving frame, a member under a dolly or one walking straight in depth does not skate and a glide the camera follows does (PR 2's moving-world gate stays only where there is no world floor); the hold layer's floor reads it before a carrier's stance; `an world show` prints each member's floor; no placement moves, and a world without `ground` lowers byte-identically. The floor is level under a rolled camera (members do not roll), keyed where the member's passes are and thinned to the travel's `tolerance_px`; a member whose hand `stage.at` wins gets none; the mark is read only on a framed shot, replaced or dropped there with a `ground` warning when hand-written and refused by the compiler elsewhere; `support`/`skate` do not measure an entity at an unseen instant (alpha at most `UNSEEN_ALPHA`).
